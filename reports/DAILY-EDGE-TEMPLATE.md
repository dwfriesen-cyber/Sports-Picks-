## Daily report — one fixed structure

1. **Yesterday's scoreboard:** final results and grades for actual published NHL/NFL selections; concise overall and top-25% W-L and %, V8-lite shadow where relevant. No unrelated historical games represented as yesterday's predictions.
2. **Model Battle:** retain **one** established graph showing overall and top-25% accuracy for original NHL V6/V7-A, historical published selections and V8-lite; NFL V1/V3 when authentic. State sampling window and retrospective vs frozen caveat **once**. Do not add duplicate top-quarter charts/tables or a separate 'genuine prospective record' section. The technical audit remains in GitHub.
3. **NHL picks:** all games, V6 winner in descending **V7-A** rank with date/Saskatchewan puck-drop, ranked confidence score (NOT calibrated win probability), top-quarter star, indicative moneyline and source when available. Surface independent model disagreements. Explain important goalie/injury/rest factors if verified.
4. **NFL picks:** all upcoming games in descending **authentic V3** confidence where available; otherwise original V1 winner ordered by absolute V1 margin and plainly labeled provisional. Show Sask kickoff, strength, top-quarter designation, sourced odds, relevant injury/news caveats. For Week 7 2026 user's confidence pool, **Chicago Bears Thursday win is locked at 10 points**; remaining 13 games occupy points 14,13,12,11,9,8,7,6,5,4,3,2,1. Do not incorrectly reuse 10.
5. **Billy Walters Value Watch:** market disagreements, promising lines/underdogs and Pass/Watch/Value verdict. No positive-EV assertion without calibrated probabilities, appropriate source and odds.
6. **Assistant hypothetical Daily $2 Ticket:** at least **two games**, may cross sports, publish independently of owner's wagers. Every ticket shows each selection and actual market type (ML vs spread), per-leg odds/book/source/timestamp, combined odds, CAD $2 stake, potential **total return including stake**, net profit, and boost if any. If odds provisional, label and adjust on confirmed bet, preserving original quote and all ticket versions. Preserve precise original selection, revision timestamp and whether truly frozen; do not backdate later drafts or count revisions as multiple tickets.
7. **Ticket-ledger performance:** yesterday's active ticket result, pending count, cumulative settled wins/losses, winning %, total hypothetical stakes, settled returns, net P/L, ROI on settled stakes, and separately initialized hypothetical bankroll. Read the actual assistant ledger; `N/A` for zero settled rather than inventing 0%. Real PlayNow bets have an **independent** ledger and summary (winning %, settled stakes/returns/net, actual balance and pending stakes), never added into hypothetical results. Note historical PlayNow figures are sensitive; only display when owner-authorized and relevant.
8. **You vs. Me:** the owner's **established 4–1 head-to-head lead** (80% of five decisions in owner's favor) from prior dialogue, with evidence/source qualification until original per-game reconciliation located. Distinguish this head-to-head competition from model prospective win %, market selections, and betting-ticket success rate. Don't invent subsequent decisions; preserve owner's dated Florida-over-Ottawa preference as a personal selection. Show leaderboard, change since yesterday, and updated results when evidenced.
